Rubik's Cube Solver

Capture or edit the cube → solve → follow each step in 3D

1. Set up the cube

Pick a face tab, hold that face flat in frame, capture it. Repeat for all six.

Or click a sticker on the 3D cube to cycle its color. Letter = color: U white, R red, F green, D yellow, L orange, B blue.

2. Solve & follow the steps

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. The cube updates on every step — turn your real cube to match.
